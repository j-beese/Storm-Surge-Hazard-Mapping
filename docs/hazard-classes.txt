Hazard Categories (Storm Surge Severity – City of Hamburg)

Category 1 — ≤ 5.0 m
Represents low-severity storm surge conditions. Areas inundated under moderate water levels.

Category 2 — ≤ 5.5 m
Represents medium-severity storm surge conditions. Inundation extends into additional low-lying areas.

Category 3 — ≤ 6.5 m
Represents high-severity storm surge conditions. Significant inundation affecting larger parts of Hamburg.

Category 4 — < 7.3 m
Represents very high-severity storm surge conditions. Extreme storm surge scenarios with widespread inundation.

Hamburg’s most severe recorded storm surge occurred in 1976, reaching a peak water level of 6.45 m above NHN at St. Pauli. The event caused extensive flooding in low-lying districts such as Wilhelmsburg and Veddel and resulted in significant damage to infrastructure. This historical benchmark corresponds to a Category 3–4 storm surge under Hamburg’s official severity guidelines and illustrates the real-world relevance of the hazard classes used in this project.
